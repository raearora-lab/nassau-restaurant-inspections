    Sources and methods

    About the data

    This site makes the New York State Department of Health's public inspection records for Nassau County food service establishments searchable by place.

    Sources

    • Food Service Establishment Inspections: Beginning 2005 (dataset 2hcc-shji), for active establishments.
    • Food Service Establishment Inspections, Inactive Establishments: Beginning 2005 (dataset aaxz-j6pj), for closed and inactive establishments.
    • Food Service Establishment: Last Inspection (dataset cnih-y5dw), used only to fill in details for matching active establishments.

    All three are published by NYS DOH on health.data.ny.gov and were downloaded on November 9 and 12, 2026. The records run from January 2001 through November 6, 2026. Records before 2008 are sparse.

    How to read it

    • One source row is one violation, not one visit. We grouped rows into inspections by establishment, date and inspection type, and counted each inspection's violation totals once.
    • Critical violations are those NYS DOH classifies as critical, meaning they are more likely to contribute to foodborne illness. Inspectors often record that these were corrected during the visit.
    • Letter grades are not an official field. NYS DOH says this data was not collected as a grading system. Where an inspector's comments say something like "Grade A", we show that letter. A missing grade means none was written in the comments. It does not mean the place was ungraded or did poorly.
    • Not only restaurants. The records cover every permitted food service operation, including delis, bakeries, caterers, school cafeterias, day care kitchens, camps and other institutions.
    • Closed places are included. Closed establishments come from the inactive dataset. A new permit at the same address or facility code shows up under "Same location history". That can mean an ownership or name change, or a second kitchen at a school, hotel or venue.
    • Map locations come from coordinates in the source data where present. Otherwise they come from another record at the same address, or from a public address locator. A small number of places could not be placed. They can still be found by search.
    • "Last 3 years" means inspections on or after November 9, 2023.

    Disclaimer

    This site republishes public government records as provided. The records may contain errors or omissions. An inspection is a snapshot of conditions on one day and does not describe how a place operates today. Do not use this site as the sole basis for any decision about a business. For official information, contact the Nassau County Department of Health or see the NYS DOH datasets linked above.
